How it works

Place
Tap an empty square or one of your own to add an atom.
Critical
Corners hold 1, edges 2, the middle 3. One more and the square explodes.
Explode
Each neighbour receives an atom and turns your colour. Chains follow.
Win
Be the last colour left on the grid.

Atari ST original

Atoms by B. Hewlett

Freeware strategy game for the Atari ST, catalogued around 1989, for 2 to 4 players with the mouse.

Amiga version

Atoms by Tom Kuhn

Written in 68000 assembly and based on the ST version by B. Hewlett. Spread through the PD scene and the Amiga Format cover disks, and still on Aminet.

Also on the Amiga

Atoms by John Girvin (1992)

A separate Amiga conversion of the same ST game, now open source.

This remake

Atoms! for browsers

New code and graphics, written from the published rules. No original code or artwork is used.
